Summary

Generations in Jazz Inc organises an annual multi-day jazz event held in South Australia. The event weekend (listed for 30 April–2 May 2026) includes competitions for individuals, sectional groups and superbands, ticketed public performances and related event programming. The organisation publishes competition results for individual, sectional and superband categories and maintains an online community and social media updates to share event information. Activities centre on providing performance and competition opportunities for aspiring and student musicians and on presenting concerts and programming for jazz audiences and ticketed attendees.
