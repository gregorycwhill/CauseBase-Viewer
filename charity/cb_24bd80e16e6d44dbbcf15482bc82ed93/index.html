Summary

Democracy in Colour Limited is an organisation based in Victoria that runs campaigns, training and youth programs focused on racial justice and related issues. Its work includes a Climate Justice Program, anti‑racism training (including workplace training), the Thriving in Colour youth leadership and campaigning program for People of Colour (POC) youth, and the Racism Out This campaign (started with Racism Out Of Politics and Racism Out Of Media). The organisation issues media statements and public communications and describes partnering with other civil society organisations. Its stated theory of change emphasises building movement power, advocating for policy reform and working in partnership to challenge discriminatory systems affecting Black, Indigenous and People of Colour (BIPOCs).
