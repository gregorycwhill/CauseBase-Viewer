Summary

Australian Christian Channel Pty Ltd operates the streaming television and on‑demand service branded as GOOD from Queensland and online. GOOD provides a downloadable app for iOS and Android and is available via web and a range of connected TV platforms; the service publishes programme listings and short articles organised into categories such as Faith and Culture, Entertainment & The Arts, Lifestyle, Families and Health and Wellness. The organisation offers a newsletter sign‑up for programme updates and news. The primary user group is people who access or view the GOOD streaming service and its website content, including families identified as a content category.
