Summary

The Russian Imperial Order of Saint Michael Archangel is a dynastic order that operates an Australian Division with a registered location in Victoria. It collects membership contributions—identified on its site as a Foundation Contribution, an Annual Oblation and branch collections—to fund the Order's running costs and to support ‘humanitarian activities’ and charitable works designated by Her Imperial Highness through the Australian Division Office. Branches may keep funds for local operating expenses and local charity support, with 25% of branch collections remitted to the Australian Division Office. The organisation maintains governance documents and an Australian Division office, and holds investiture ceremonies that include an Orthodox moleben (liturgical service). It offers membership sign-up and public investiture services.
