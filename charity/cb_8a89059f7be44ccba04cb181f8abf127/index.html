Summary

Australian National Capital Artists Inc (ANCA) is an artist-run gallery organisation based at 1 Rosevear Place, Dickson, Australian Capital Territory. As of mid-2026 it operates a physical gallery with posted public hours (Fri–Sun 11–4pm, Thursdays by appointment) and contact details, runs scheduled exhibitions with opening events, and publishes exhibition details and artist statements on its website. Recent programming listed an exhibition titled Everyday. Reverence (3–26 July) that named seven exhibiting artists and described individual works. Stated beneficiaries include the named exhibiting artists and people who attend or visit listed exhibitions (gallery visitors).
