Summary

Global Wildlife Fund is an Australian charity that partners with local conservation organisations to support projects to restore wildlife populations and habitats in selected high‑priority areas. It works in Sub‑Saharan Africa (including Matusadona National Park, Zimbabwe), the Andes–Amazon (including Narupa Reserve, Ecuador), New Guinea and the Pacific (Vogelkop Peninsula, Papua, Indonesia) and Asian biodiversity hotspots. The organisation provides funding for wildlife translocations (for example black rhino and sable antelope), helps acquire or expand private non‑profit reserves (for example a 250 ha Narupa Reserve expansion), supports community forest and land‑rights work in Papua, and negotiates project funding agreements while tracking progress and reporting on project outcomes. Directors and founders donate their time to research, assess, support and audit projects, and Australians can make tax‑deductible gifts to support specific projects.
