Summary

Geelong & District Day Nursery Incorporated is a local early‑childhood service based in Geelong, Victoria. It operates a day nursery / children’s centre, providing on‑site care and early childhood education for children in the Geelong & district area. Regulatory registration and the organisation’s website and annual information statement identify the organisation, its role as a children’s centre and its location; financial filings are available for the 2022–23 reporting year. CharityGraph has evidence that the organisation runs a day nursery in Geelong but does not have sufficient descriptive evidence in this release to identify specific service models, age ranges served, opening hours or eligibility criteria.
