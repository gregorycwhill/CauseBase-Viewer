Summary

The Trustee for Invasive Species Solutions Trust, trading as the Centre for Invasive Species Solutions, is an Australian national centre based in the ACT that coordinates collaborative invasive-species research, development and engagement. It hosts and maintains national digital platforms for weed and pest information and tools (weeds.org.au; weedscan.org.au; pestsmart.org.au; feralscan.org.au), coordinates LandSmart Services nationally, develops and disseminates national codes of practice, training and resources, and publishes reports such as the WeedRemeed Impact Report 2024–25. Its stated work targets invasive species that affect wildlife (including threatened native species) and supports community members and land managers through online tools, training, resources and public events.
