Summary

South Australian Rainbow Advocacy Alliance Inc. (SARAA) is a South Australia‑based peak body that advocates for LGBTIQA+ people across the state. As of the cited sources it runs policy advocacy and submissions (for example on births, deaths and marriages regulations), correspondence with health authorities about gender‑affirming care, and public events and commemorations such as Trans Day of Visibility and youth forums. SARAA provides online resources including a “Know Your Rights” guide, operates a Rainbow Local Council Project to support local government inclusion, and recruits members and sells branded merchandise. Recent activity listed on the organisation’s site includes submissions, letters to government, event listings and involvement in demographic question reviews for the state Department of Health and Wellbeing.
