Summary

5D Clinics Foundation Ltd is a Western Australia–based foundation that aims to provide access to cancer treatment for everyone. It describes two core activities: consulting with clinical experts to identify treatment options tailored to individual patients, and providing financial assistance to help patients access those treatments. The foundation states a mission to move patients beyond fear of the unknown and closer to confidence when diagnosed with cancer. Stated beneficiaries are people diagnosed with cancer (patients).
