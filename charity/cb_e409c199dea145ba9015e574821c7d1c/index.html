Summary

4LIFESKILLS INC. is an Australian not-for-profit based in Rockingham, Western Australia that provides personalised support services for young people and adults living with disability. In the organisation’s 2022–23 reporting period it describes one-to-one and daily supports focused on building independence and social connections, creative and leisure activities such as pottery sessions in Rockingham, assistance to enable participants to exhibit or share artwork, and short-breaks or holiday support options. The organisation also engages with corporate sponsors. Services are described as goal‑focused and tailored to individual aims such as employment, independent living and social participation.
