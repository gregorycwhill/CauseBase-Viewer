Summary

K2C Incorporated (Kosciuszko to Coast, K2C) is a partnership of fourteen organisations that delivers natural resource management and environment services across the Kosciuszko to Coast region of south‑east New South Wales and to rural lessees in the Australian Capital Territory. K2C runs a Land for Wildlife property registration and assessment program, delivers projects such as “Being a Good Neighbour to the Bush for the Yass Gorge”, and runs workshops on habitat and threatened species (including Scarlet Robin) under the Saving Our Species framework. It organises public events and site visits (examples: Feeling Fishy Day, Maffra Lake TSR) and publishes local fauna news (items on quolls and arboreal mammals). Partners work with land owners, landcare groups and local community members to support on‑ground natural resource management and habitat activities.
