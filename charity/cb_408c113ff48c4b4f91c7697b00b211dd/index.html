Summary

As of August 2026, the Defence Force Welfare Association – National Inc (DFWA) describes itself as an independent voice for Australian Defence Force members, veterans and their families. It advocates to government on ADF pay and conditions of service, rehabilitation of injured veterans, compensation for service injuries, retirement and superannuation, service delivery and veteran recognition. DFWA publishes a regular newsletter and the Camaraderie magazine, runs surveys to inform its WRA 2026–29 submission (including pre‑offer and post‑offer surveys), and is funded by branch membership fees and donations. The organisation invites branch membership, newsletter subscriptions and survey participation to help shape its submissions and communications.
