Summary

AGENCY PROJECTS LIMITED (Agency Projects) is a Victoria‑registered arts organisation that delivers First Nations‑led exhibitions, commissions and related public programming on local, national and international scales. Its work includes curating and staging exhibitions that centre Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander artists, art and built‑environment commissions, forums and the UNTOLD talks series, digital initiatives such as the UPLANDS mapping resource and artist portraits, a residency program (Agency AiR) for First Nations participants, and publications under Agency Editions. Agency also runs events and conference programming (including NAIDOC and Garma partnerships) and says it partners with government and overseas institutions for international projects. Stated beneficiaries include Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander artists, curators and cultural leaders, First Nations participants and communities, and audiences and collectors.
