Summary

Aboriginal And Torres Strait Islander Healing Foundation Limited (The Healing Foundation) is a national Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander organisation registered in the Australian Capital Territory. It works with Stolen Generations survivors, their families and broader Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander communities to support survivor‑led, community‑led healing and to restore cultural connections. Its activities include community healing projects, a grants program for truth‑telling projects, production and dissemination of resources (fact sheets, reports, toolkits and videos), online events and public forums, and advocacy with government and policy makers about trauma‑aware, healing‑informed practice. The organisation also publishes policy material and maintains a library of resources for practitioners, survivors and other audiences.
