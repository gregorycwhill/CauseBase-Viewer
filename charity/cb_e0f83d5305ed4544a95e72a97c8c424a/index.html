Summary

Angel Gowns Australia Incorporated is a volunteer-run Australian not-for-profit that handcrafts and distributes garments made from donated wedding dresses. It supplies Angel Gown garments and smaller micro-gowns, tutus made from salvaged tulle, and Early Loss Pouches to hospitals, funeral homes and directly to families. Volunteers act as seamstresses, tutu makers, de‑constructors and coordinators; the charity also manages logistics, storage, special garment requests and a database to coordinate supply. Angel Gowns Australia reports nationwide activity and volunteer membership, and its 2020–21 annual report states it was servicing 211 hospitals and 102 funeral homes across Australia.
