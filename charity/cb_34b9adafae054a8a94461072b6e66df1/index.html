Summary

Catholic Church Insurance Limited is a Victoria‑registered insurer operating in Australia. It provides customer‑facing insurance administration, including claims handling, issuing certificates of currency, accepting policy payments and online policy management via a customer portal. The organisation publishes information on financial hardship and maintains news and media statements, including a notice referencing subscription to the General Insurance Code of Practice and an update titled “orderly run‑off of CCI business” (May 2023). Regulatory filings include an Australian Information Statement for the 2022–23 year. Evidence for these activities and communications comes from the organisation’s website and regulator records cited below.
