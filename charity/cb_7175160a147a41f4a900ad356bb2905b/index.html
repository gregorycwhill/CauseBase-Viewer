Summary

Ivory's Rock Foundation is a Queensland-based foundation that reports activity across Australia, New Zealand and the South Pacific. As of 2026 it supports the Peace Education Program in Australia, New Zealand and the South Pacific and sponsors and hosts peace and wellbeing events in its region. The foundation describes the Peace Education Program as being delivered in high schools, universities, health-care facilities, veterans' groups, police units and correctional facilities. It states that Prem Rawat has been invited as keynote speaker for selected events when his schedule permits and that it supports activities associated with the United Nations International Day of Peace. The foundation also reports conservation and sustainability work at the Ivory's Rock property and records over 160 species, including some threatened or endangered wildlife.
