Summary

Innovate Australia Ltd is a not-for-profit facilitator of Australian innovation, established in 2014 and based in Western Australia; the organisation states it has a wide national footprint across Australia. It works with industry to encourage, assist and promote Australian innovation, develop innovation programs and networks, and showcase innovation through events and sponsorship. Innovate Australia also judges startup competitions and publishes online commentary — a June 9, 2026 post described the organisation’s experience judging AI startups. The organisation describes itself as independent and focused on bringing about and encouraging innovation through industry-facing programs, events and online publishing.
