Summary

Asia Pacific Diocese of Malankara (Indian) Orthodox Syrian Church Ltd is a diocesan body of the Malankara Orthodox Syrian Church headquartered in Canberra, Australian Capital Territory. The diocese has jurisdiction across the Asia & Pacific region, including Australia, New Zealand, Malaysia and Singapore, and was established effective 1 November 2024. It lists more than 2,000 members and 20+ parishes. Day‑to‑day work described on its site includes oversight of parishes and diocesan ministries, maintenance of priestly appointments and parish structures, and publication of news and a newsletter alongside organisational contact details and notices.
