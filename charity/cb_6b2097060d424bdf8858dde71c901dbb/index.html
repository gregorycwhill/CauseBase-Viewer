Summary

International China Concern (Australia) Ltd is an organisation registered in Victoria, Australia. Available materials include a website with a privacy policy describing collection of IP addresses, use of cookies, SSL encryption for transactions and links to third‑party payment providers, and a complaints policy that applies to members of the public, volunteers, staff and board members. The complaints policy outlines seven handling stages (acknowledgement, assessment, planning, investigation, response, review and systemic follow‑up), refers to biblical principles as guiding principles, and notes volunteers as a stakeholder group. The organisation also references a mission and vision page. CharityGraph does not have sufficient descriptive evidence in this release to independently identify specific activities delivered or the intended beneficiary groups.
