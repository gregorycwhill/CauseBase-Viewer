Summary

Aged Care Deloraine Inc is a not‑for‑profit provider of aged care services in Deloraine, Tasmania (information current in evidence observed in 2026). It operates two residential care facilities — Kanangra (48 beds) and Grenoch (47 beds) — alongside independent living units for people wishing to downscale. Services described include occasional respite care, specialised high‑care services including dementia care, and admissions processes with individualised clinical care planning supported by an Admissions Officer and Clinical Care Coordinator. The organisation reports community links in Deloraine, hosts work placements for local students, and offers site tours and admissions consultations for prospective residents and families.
