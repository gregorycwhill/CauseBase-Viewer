Summary

Spiritualists National Union Of WA Inc is a spiritualist centre based in Western Australia. As of August 2026 it publishes a mediums schedule for regular services, runs a learning programme that includes spiritualism and mediumship training, and hosts gatherings where people share life experiences and explore spiritual growth. The organisation describes its attendees as "like-minded people" and invites contact for information or locations of its church. The available records identify these programmatic activities and participation routes (attendance at scheduled services and medium events, and participation in the learning programme) but do not provide detailed breakdowns of service locations or participant numbers in this release.
