Summary

Celebrate Health Care Limited is an aged care provider operating in rural northeast and east‑coast Tasmania, serving communities in the Glamorgan Spring Bay, Dorset and Break O’Day regions. It operates two residential aged care homes in Scottsdale and Swansea (branded May Shaw and Aminya) and offers short‑term respite stays at those sites. The organisation also delivers personalised in‑home aged care support and manages Consumer Directed Care (CDC) packages. Website material and client testimonials describe organised activities and bus tours for residents. These services are presented as local, community‑facing aged care and support across the named Tasmanian regions.
