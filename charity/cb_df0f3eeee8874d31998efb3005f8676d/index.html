Summary

AEIOU Foundation is an Australian charity that provided early intervention services for children aged 2–6 with an autism diagnosis. As shown on its website and in its 2023 regulatory filing, AEIOU describes itself as neurodiversity‑affirming and says it uses a combination of person‑first and identity‑first language based on feedback from a 2023 parent community study. The organisation lists a Brisbane office (Level 1, 15 Astor Terrace, Spring Hill, QLD), phone and email contact channels, weekday office hours, and secure online family payment functionality. AEIOU states it is developing a Reconciliation Action Plan. The organisation’s website also includes a notice issued by the liquidators referring to “AEIOU FOUNDATION (In Liquidation).”
