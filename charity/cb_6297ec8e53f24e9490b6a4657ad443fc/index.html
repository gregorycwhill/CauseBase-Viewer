Summary

Castlemaine Art Museum is a regional art museum in Castlemaine, Victoria, located on Dja Dja Wurrung / Jaara country. Founded in 1913, it operates a public gallery with free entry Thursday–Sunday and a programme of temporary and scheduled exhibitions. The museum maintains and presents a collection of Australian art, First Nations cultural material and historical artefacts, and runs public events such as openings, artist talks, performances and panel tours. It hosts school group visits, commissions and pays local artists for talks and workshops, and operates a museum shop selling limited-edition artworks. The museum supports its activity through memberships, donations and supporter programs and describes supporter-funded uses including wall labels, artist fees, installation and school group visits.
