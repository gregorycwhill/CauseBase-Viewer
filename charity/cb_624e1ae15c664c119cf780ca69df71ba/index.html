Summary

ACC TAS MINISTRIES LTD (operating as Youth Alive) is an Australian organisation that frames its purpose in religious language, saying it exists so that every young person in Australia “lives their full God purpose.” Its website offers an email sign-up for news and updates and includes a partners section. The organisation is described as operating in Australia.
