Summary

Calvary Retirement Community Ryde Limited is a Calvary Health Care provider based in Ryde, New South Wales (evidence current to 2026). It operates health and aged‑care services in the locality, including hospitals, residential aged care, retirement living and home care. The organisation publishes clinical safety and quality data and maintains careers and recruitment information. It supports volunteer programmes and auxiliaries and runs public events for residents, including the Calvary Residential Aged Care Games. Its stated mission includes providing healthcare to vulnerable people, including those reaching the end of life.
