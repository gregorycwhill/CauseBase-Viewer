Summary

Befriend Inc. is a community organisation operating in Greater Perth, Western Australia, including Wanneroo and Perth’s northern corridor. It describes its purpose as sparking connections and supporting people who want a greater sense of belonging. Befriend runs weekly community groups and gatherings (examples include John’s WingSpan Group and Nat’s Art Circle), delivers workshops and courses, provides guidance and mentoring and runs a multi‑week Possibility Fellowship programme. The organisation organises local projects and public events such as the Swan Repair Café, Burns Beach Sunset Walk and the Neil Hawkins BBQ, and publishes articles, podcasts and a Social Impact Report. Services and activities are presented for people seeking social connection and belonging, people with disability (examples cited include autism and expressive aphasia) and residents of Greater Perth.
