Summary

Regional Australia Institute (RAI) is a national think tank focused on issues affecting regional Australia. As of August 2026, RAI produces research and data products (for example the Regional Movers Index developed with the Commonwealth Bank, the Regional Labour Markets Update, and the Regionalisation Ambition 2032 progress reports), develops policy and advocacy material (Policy Pulse), and runs events and public programming including the Regions Rising National Summit, Regions Rising WA and member-only Dialogue Day. RAI also offers an executive education Masterclass program for CEOs and executives and delivers partnership projects with external organisations. Its activities are aimed at residents of regional Australia, community, industry and government leaders who use its research and attend its events, and CEOs and executives who enrol in its education programs. (Sources: RAI website; ACNC AIS 2023; ACNC register.)
