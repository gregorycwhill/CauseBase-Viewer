Summary

The Corporation Of The Trustees Of The Order Of The Sisters Of Mercy In Queensland is the corporate entity for the Sisters of Mercy, Brisbane Congregation. It operates in Queensland, with activity linked to Brisbane and Aubigny. When supported, the organisation publishes news and announcements on its website and organises commemorative gatherings, farewell events and anniversary celebrations for congregation members. Examples recorded include a 70‑year jubilee for Sister Catharine Courtney (Sister Mary Benedict), a farewell to Robyn Rodwell and a 130‑year service commemoration. Recorded modes of participation include attending public gatherings and reading website news. CharityGraph does not have sufficient descriptive evidence in this release to identify other service programs, beneficiaries beyond congregation members, or operational details such as staffing or finances.
