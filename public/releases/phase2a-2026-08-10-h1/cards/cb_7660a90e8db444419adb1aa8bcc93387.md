## CauseBase summary

According to the organisation's website, Alcoholics Anonymous – Perth (Aa Service Council For Western Australia) runs a 24‑hour helpline (08 9325 3566 or 1300 22 22 22) and maintains an office at Claisebrook Lotteries House, 33 Moore Street, East Perth, with listed opening hours Monday–Friday 9.00am–4.30pm. The site publishes meeting lists and a calendar of events for Perth and multiple WA districts (Area A, B, C; Hills; Mid‑West; North‑West; Peel; Port; South West) and links to online and international meetings. The website describes open meetings that are available to anyone interested in AA’s programme, notes that non‑alcoholics may attend, and states the only requirement for membership is “a desire to stop drinking.” It offers resources for people new to AA (pamphlets, personal stories, illustrated talks and recovery workshops), a page for fly‑in/fly‑out workers with peer contacts, and a listing for “Serenity” magazine and other literature. The site also posts a safety policy, code of conduct, a safeguarding statement and an incident report form. Contact email AAPerthoffice@aaperthwa.org is given and links are provided to national AA resources and an international meetings app.
