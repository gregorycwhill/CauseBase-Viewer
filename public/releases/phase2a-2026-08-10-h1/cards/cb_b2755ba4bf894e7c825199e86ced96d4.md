## CauseBase summary

According to its website and ACNC filings, Befriend Inc. is a Western Australian community organisation that describes its work as sparking connections and supporting people who want a greater sense of belonging. The website lists recurring activities including weekly community groups and gatherings (for example John’s WingSpan Group and Nat’s Art Circle), workshops and courses, guidance and mentoring, and a Possibility Fellowship program. It also describes locally‑focused projects such as the Swan Repair Café, Burns Beach Sunset Walk and Neil Hawkins BBQ, and a Resilient Together Wanneroo project in Perth’s northern corridor. Befriend publishes articles, podcasts and an annual Social Impact Report; the site notes a 2026 Inclusive Volunteering Award and various member stories. The organisation’s ACNC data and 2022–23 AIS show revenue of AUD 895,313 and expenses of AUD 976,329 for the year to 30 June 2023. The website refers repeatedly to hosts, volunteers and a Community Connector role that help run activities.
