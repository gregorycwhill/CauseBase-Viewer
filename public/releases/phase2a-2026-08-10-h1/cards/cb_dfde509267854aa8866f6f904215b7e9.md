## CauseBase summary

The Australian Conservation Foundation (ACF) presents itself on its website as a national advocacy organisation campaigning on climate, deforestation, fossil fuel projects and species decline. Website content cites petitions and open letters (for example, an open letter to Hungry Jack’s on deforestation-free beef and a “No new gas” petition), fundraising appeals to support legal action (a call to “power our court case against Whitehaven Coal”), and a submission sent to the Federal Environment Minister accompanied by 19,516 signatures. The site promotes public events and member activities, including “Australia’s Biggest Bushwalk” and a wildlife art competition for children aged 5–12, and invites people to “join a local group”, “be a member” and “volunteer with us.” The homepage lists news items about courts, government bills and industry finance. The ACNC/AIS extract for 2022–23 records revenue of AUD 18,128,096 and total expenses of AUD 19,263,764 for the reporting period. Claims about campaign “wins” and other outcomes are presented on the website as organisation-authored statements; the evidence provided here is limited to the organisation’s website snapshot and the ACNC/AIS financial extract.
