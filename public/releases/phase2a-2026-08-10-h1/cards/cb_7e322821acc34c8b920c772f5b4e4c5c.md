## CauseBase summary

Adelaide Symphony Orchestra (ASO) presents a year-round programme of public concerts, regional touring, learning and community activity based in South Australia. The ASO’s 2022 annual report lists 53 performances across series including the Symphony Series (Adelaide Town Hall), Sanctuary Series (Grainger Studio), Chamber and Matinée series (Elder Hall), and special events at Festival Theatre. The company reports regional touring to mid‑north towns including Peterborough and Clare and collaborations with the Adelaide Festival, WOMADelaide, State Opera and The Australian Ballet. Education and participation work recorded in the reports includes school incursions, a commissioned children’s piece based on Alison Lester’s Are We There Yet?, community centre concerts in association with Community Centres SA, and a mini‑festival She Speaks with film, symposium, chamber events and premieres of works by women composers. The ASO also documents digital programming (a Virtual Concert Hall, online mini‑recitals and the Silos & Symphonies learning project) and an artist‑led inclusive project called Floods of Fire described as community‑building and addressing environment and climate themes. The ASO’s materials acknowledge Kaurna Country and note the role of volunteers, donors and supporters in company activity.
