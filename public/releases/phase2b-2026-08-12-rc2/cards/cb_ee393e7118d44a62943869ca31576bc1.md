## CauseBase summary

Vital Life Choice Ltd is a registered not‑for‑profit charity based in Adelaide, South Australia (Henley Beach Rd, Lockleys), with a listed research partner in Darwin. It delivers community health counselling focused on the prevention and control of diabetes, describing its core activity as a Community Health Centre operation mainly providing counselling. The organisation produces and disseminates educational resources that summarise peer‑reviewed medical and scientific research on Type 2 diabetes, diabetic complications and related conditions, and plans public awareness campaigns using TV, radio, live events and digital media to promote early detection, lifestyle change and effective management strategies. Vital Life Choice invites volunteers and partners and states its materials may be freely shared for non‑commercial disease prevention purposes.
