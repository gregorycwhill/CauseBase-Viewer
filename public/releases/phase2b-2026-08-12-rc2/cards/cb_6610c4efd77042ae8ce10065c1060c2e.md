## CauseBase summary

Biopixel Oceans Foundation Limited is an Australian marine research and education organisation operating on the Great Barrier Reef, Queensland. It tags and tracks marine animals in real time using the BioTracker platform, undertakes applied research on marine megafauna (including shark behaviour) and provides ecological information for fisheries management. The foundation maintains Oceanpedia, a multimedia library of short Great Barrier Reef video clips, produces videos and participates in streamed documentaries and media partnerships, and publishes journal articles and research collaborations. It also offers naming/sponsorship opportunities for individually tagged animals and reports tracking more than 1,000 animals currently being followed.
