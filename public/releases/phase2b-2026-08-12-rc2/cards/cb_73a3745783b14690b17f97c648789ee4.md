## CauseBase summary

Anglican Camping Tasmania is a ministry of the Anglican Diocese of Tasmania that runs residential Christian camping programs for children and young people in grades 3–12. It operates summer and other seasonal camps at multiple sites around Tasmania, including Port Sorell, Huon, Derby, Blue Lagoon, Camp Banksia and Mayfield. The organisation runs scheduled summer camps (several listed for January 2027) and a Leaders in Training program for students in grades 9–12, including multiple LiT sessions noted for 2026.
