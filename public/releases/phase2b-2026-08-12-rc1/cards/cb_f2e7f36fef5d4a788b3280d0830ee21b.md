## CauseBase summary

Project Underdog Rescue Inc is registered in Victoria and describes itself on its website as operating a specialised veterinary service and community outreach to support animals and their guardians. The organisation’s website states it runs Melbourne’s first “100% Fear Free” certified mobile veterinary clinic and that profits from that service help fund its animal rescue work. The website also says it delivers community outreach intended to keep animals with people by providing animal care services and supplies to vulnerable community members, and that it uses its platform for education and advocacy about responsible pet guardianship. The ACNC Information Statement for 01/07/2022 to 30/06/2023 records revenue of AUD 543,901 and total expenses of AUD 679,638 for that reporting period. The descriptions of programs, clinic certification and the funding model are taken from the organisation’s website; these are presented here as the organisation states them and are not independently verified against third‑party sources.
