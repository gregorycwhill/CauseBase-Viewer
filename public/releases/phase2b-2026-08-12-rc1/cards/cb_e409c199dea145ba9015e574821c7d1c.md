## CauseBase summary

4LIFESKILLS INC. is an organisation based in Western Australia whose website states it provides personalised support services for young people and adults living with disability. The site lists a range of goal areas the organisation says it helps with, including looking after yourself and your home, building social skills and relationships, finding a rewarding career, taking short breaks or holidays, pursuing hobbies, and staying healthy and active. Website items describe specific activities for participants: a pottery session for Rockingham participants, support for an artist named Lesley to share her work, and a feature called “A Day with Gina” about daily support for independence and connections. The available evidence is primarily the organisation’s website and its ACNC reporting extract; the website frames services as personalised and person-centred but does not provide detailed participant counts or independent verification of outcomes.
