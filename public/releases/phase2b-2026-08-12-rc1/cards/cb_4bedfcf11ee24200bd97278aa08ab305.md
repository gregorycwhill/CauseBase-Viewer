## CauseBase summary

FORM Building a State of Creativity Limited (FORM) is an ACNC-registered organisation that, according to its website, “works across Western Australia to make creativity matter in communities and public spaces.” The website lists a range of activities the organisation says it delivers or supports: public art and public-space projects, arts and cultural consultancy, cultural tourism experiences, a public gallery (FORM Gallery), a studio (Spinifex Hill Studio), creative learning programmes and events. Recent website entries name a Pilbara Creatives expression-of-interest callout in Port Hedland and gallery exhibitions including “Unwavering” and “Deadly | 50 Years of NAIDOC.” The site gives a Perth office address and a phone number and includes an acknowledgement of Whadjuk Noongar and other Traditional Custodians. The ACNC information statement for 01/01/2023–30/06/2023 reports revenue of AUD 2,691,949 and expenses of AUD 2,876,757 for that reporting period; the AIS notes the period is transitional. The organisation’s web text frames its work as supporting “people and places” and a creative economy for Western Australian communities; the summary preserves those claims as organisation statements rather than independent findings.
