## CauseBase summary

Castlemaine Art Museum Ltd (website) describes itself as a regional museum founded in 1913 and located at 14 Lyttleton Street, Castlemaine VIC 3450 on Dja Dja Wurrung (Jaara) country. The website lists a permanent collection of Australian paintings, First Nations cultural material and historical artefacts and has pages labelled “View Collection (4748)” and “Artwork View Collection (2153)”. The museum’s public opening hours are Thursday–Sunday with free admission. The site publishes a rolling exhibitions program with dated shows (examples given: Biyal, Wildflowers, a Len Fox Painting Award exhibition and a Rhett D’Costa show) and a schedule of events including opening nights, artist talks, performances and panel tours. The website also describes supporter/member options and specific donor amounts with stated examples of what each amount funds (for example $50 for wall labels, $250 for artists’ talks/workshops, $500 to employ local installers, $1,000 to host several school groups, $5,000 to commission a new artwork). The organisation’s ACNC AIS 2023 extract reports revenue of AUD 586,117 and total expenses of AUD 590,747 for 01/07/2022–30/06/2023. The site operates a shop offering limited-edition artworks.
