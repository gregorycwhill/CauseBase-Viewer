## CauseBase summary

Young Voices Of Melbourne Inc is a Melbourne-based youth choral organisation. As of 2026 it operates two ensembles, the Senior Training Choir and the Performing Choir, and enrolment is by membership of those named choirs. The group runs regular rehearsals and an annual rehearsal weekend (branded Supercamp; most recently held 27 Feb–1 Mar 2026), engages visiting and associate artists and choral clinicians (including the appointment of international conductor Dr Michael Barrett as Associate Artist in April 2026) and provides artistic leadership through an Artistic Director who began in 2025.
