Banks Street Newmarket Community Preschooling Centre Inc (Banks Street Kindy) is a not-for-profit community kindergarten operating on the grounds of Newmarket State School in Newmarket, Queensland. As of 2026 it runs community kindergarten sessions with daily outdoor play and nature-based programming, including regular Bush Kindy visits to nearby Banks Street Reserve. The kindergarten runs an arts program and an annual art show, maintains gardens and chickens, and organises family-facing events. A volunteer kindy management committee runs governance and fundraising activities. The centre maintains links with Newmarket State School for shared activities and transitions, and includes acknowledgements and learning about First Nations culture in its program.
