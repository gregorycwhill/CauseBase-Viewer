## CauseBase summary

According to the organisation's website and its ACNC reporting, Banks Street Newmarket Community Preschooling Centre Inc (Banks Street Kindy) is a small not‑for‑profit community kindergarten located on the grounds of Newmarket State School in Newmarket, Queensland. The website describes educator‑led early childhood learning with daily outdoor play across multiple outdoor areas (sandpit, mud kitchen, fairy garden), regular visits to the neighbouring Banks Street Reserve (described as a “Bush Kindy” day), and on‑site chickens named Rainbow and Lamington. The site names a Kindy Director, Sharon Kemp, and an art teacher, Kath, and says the kindergarten holds an annual art show for families. The website describes a volunteer kindy management committee, fundraising activity, and connections with Newmarket State School (library visits and participation in school events). The site also describes a daily acknowledgement of Country, a Welcome to Country song, and visits from Aunty Sharron and Uncle Bruce to teach about First Nations culture. The ACNC AIS 2023 extract in the supplied evidence lists revenue of AUD 437,441 and total expenses of AUD 385,269 for the 2023 year.
