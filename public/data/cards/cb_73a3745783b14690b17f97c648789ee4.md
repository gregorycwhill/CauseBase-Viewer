## CauseBase summary

Anglican Camping Tasmania Commission (Anglican Camping Tasmania) is described on its website as a not-for-profit ministry of the Anglican Diocese of Tasmania that runs residential camping programs for children and young people. The organisation’s website lists multiple summer camps and year-round camps for school grades 3–12, with named programs including Burst (Grades 3–6), Banksia (Junior High, Grades 7–9), Derby (Senior High), Huon (Grades 10–12), Vibes (Grades 10–12), Mayfield, Spokes and Leaders in Training (LiT) for Grades 9–12; some sessions have specific dates and locations such as Port Sorell (Banksia, 2–6 Jan 2027) and Huon (3–8 Jan 2027). The website states the group has operated for over 30 years and describes a high leader-to-camper ratio, leaders who hold state government checks and “Safe Church Communities” training and endorsement. The site presents aims to provide opportunities to explore Christian faith, build friendships and offer activities not available daily; these are organisation-authored descriptions rather than independently verified facts.
