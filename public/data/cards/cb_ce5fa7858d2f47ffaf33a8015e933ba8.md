Australian LGBTQ Board and Executive Inclusion Forum Limited is a New South Wales-based organisation that promotes diversity and inclusion in corporate boardrooms. It fosters discussions between company board members, nominations committees, executive search firms, industry governance bodies and interested individuals. ALBEI organises and promotes events, maintains a subscription list and website communications, and identifies a set of priority projects related to boardroom inclusion. The organisation invites people to subscribe for event updates, send questions, offer help and attend ALBEI events. Activities described focus on convening conversations and project work around boardroom inclusion rather than direct service provision to individual clients.
