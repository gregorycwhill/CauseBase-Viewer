## CauseBase summary

Accelerated Evolution - The Break Inc. is a volunteer-run not-for-profit based in Caulfield, Melbourne, Victoria, that provides mental health and wellbeing recovery programs. It runs small-group programs such as mindfulness, anger management and relapse prevention, with groups limited to 16 participants and an intended staffing ratio of about one staff member per four participants as funding allows. The Break delivers an accredited Work & Development Permit program and facilitates Work for the Dole arrangements for people over 55. It accepts course placements and offers program participation for people experiencing homelessness, unemployment, substance recovery, trauma, psychosomatic or psychological disorders, disability, isolation and loneliness. The organisation evolved from a clinic established in St Kilda East and lists a Caulfield head office.
