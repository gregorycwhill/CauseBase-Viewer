## CauseBase summary

Generations in Jazz Inc is presented on its website as the organiser of a multi-day jazz event called Generations in Jazz, held across a weekend. A 2026 event page on the site lists “2026 Event Results” and pages for “Individual Results”, “SBA - Sectional Results”, “VEA - Sectional Results” and “Superbands”, and gives event dates of Friday 30 April to Sunday 2 May. The site text states the event brings together aspiring musicians to “learn from each other, study, and perform” and offers tickets for public sale; it also invites people to join a community mailing list and to follow the organisation on social media. Regulatory extracts in the evidence set show an ACNC register entry and an Australian Information Statement covering 01/10/2022–30/09/2023, for which the organisation reported revenue of AUD 1,755,970 and total expenses of AUD 1,591,204. The available sources are the organisation’s website snapshot and ACNC filings; programme details beyond the weekend event, operational staffing, geographic reach outside South Australia, and participant numbers are not provided in the supplied evidence.
