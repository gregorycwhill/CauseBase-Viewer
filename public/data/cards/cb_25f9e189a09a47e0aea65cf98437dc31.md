Mighty Livingwaters - Life Fellowship, trading as Good Success Church, is a Christian church based in Jandakot, Western Australia. It holds weekly public worship services on Sundays at 10:00am and 6:30pm at its Jandakot location and offers a livestream of services. The church runs age‑based ministries and fellowships including Men’s Ministry, Daughters of the King, Impact (18+), Step In (13–17), Children’s Church, Home Altar and Connect Groups, and publishes a fortnightly podcast. Services involve praise, worship and teaching, and the congregation meets for social interaction such as morning tea. Evidence indicates these activities are delivered in person in Jandakot and online via livestream and podcast.
