## CauseBase summary

Mighty Livingwaters - Life Fellowship (trading as Good Success Church) is presented on its website as a Christian church based in Jandakot, Western Australia. According to the organisation's website, it holds weekly public worship services in Jandakot on Sundays at 10:00am and 6:30pm (with a livestream option) and runs a range of ministries described as Men’s Ministry, Daughters of the King, Impact (18+ years), Step In (13–17 years), Home Altar, Connect Groups and Children’s Church. The website also describes fortnightly podcast episodes and social interaction around morning tea and coffee after services. The ACNC information included in the AIS for 01/07/2022–30/06/2023 reports revenue of AUD 1,098,206 and total expenses of AUD 833,466 for that period. The ACNC register extract is available for the organisation. The material here combines the organisation’s published schedule and ministry list with the AIS financial figures; program reach beyond Jandakot and details of staffing or volunteer arrangements are not provided in the supplied evidence.
