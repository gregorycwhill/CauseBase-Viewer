# Katherine Isolated Children's Service Inc

- CauseBase ID: cb_59546495c16f451b8b3792ae18771762
- Dataset version: 0.5.0-2026-08-15
- Contract version: 0.5

Katherine Isolated Children's Service Inc (KICS) is a Northern Territory organisation that delivers playgroups for children who are socially or geographically isolated across a reported service area of about 760,000 square kilometres. Each KICS Field Team is composed of a playgroup leader, a trained or experienced early childhood educator and a playgroup support worker; teams travel to communities to run playgroups. KICS also maintains an office that handles orders and enquiries and sells EcoViva mats as a fundraising activity; mats are available for pickup at the office or can be delivered by Field Teams. The organisation reports funding from the National Indigenous Australians Agency and from private donations.
