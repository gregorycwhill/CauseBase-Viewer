## CauseBase summary

Abounding Life Foundation (branded around Nader Mansour) is an Australia-based online Christian ministry with international reach. It publishes practical Bible teaching through video sermons and lectures, structured online courses marketed as "My Bible Academy", written study materials and audio sermons/podcasts. The site includes a user registration and course enrolment system, invites users to submit testimonies, and provides an online donation option labelled "Give." The organisation presents its content as aimed at helping people read, understand and apply the Bible; it targets Christians and church leaders who use online teaching resources and encourages participation by watching videos, enrolling in courses, reading materials and listening to audio resources.
