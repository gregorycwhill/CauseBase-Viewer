## CauseBase summary

Project Underdog Rescue Inc is a Melbourne-based animal welfare and rescue organisation. When supported by evidence observed 10 August 2026, it operates a 100% Fear Free certified mobile veterinary clinic in Melbourne that prioritises animals’ emotional wellbeing; provides community outreach that supplies animal care services and supplies to vulnerable pet owners to help keep animals with their families during hardship; undertakes animal rescue and life‑saving work; and delivers education and advocacy about responsible pet guardianship via its public platform and social media. The organisation states that profits from its Fear Free vet service help fund its rescue work.
