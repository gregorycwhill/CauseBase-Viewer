## CauseBase summary

Australian Conservation Foundation Incorporated is an environmental advocacy organisation registered in Victoria that campaigns nationally across Australia. It runs public campaigns on climate, nature and land-use issues using petitions, open letters and submissions to federal decision-makers. The organisation raises funds to support legal action in environmental cases, organises public programs such as bushwalks and a children’s wildlife art competition, and recruits and supports local volunteer groups. It also promotes targeted corporate and government actions (for example on deforestation and fossil-fuel projects) and solicits signatures for parliamentary and ministerial submissions.
