## CauseBase summary

Arts Nexus Inc is a not‑for‑profit, community-based arts organisation operating in Far North Queensland and based in Atherton, Queensland. It was founded in 1995 and incorporated in May 1996 as an umbrella body for arts, cultural and creative industries in the FNQ region. The organisation describes work to strengthen operational capacity across the regional arts sector, to drive policy and regional strategic planning, and to support artists, cultural practitioners and creative industries with the business aspects of arts making. Arts Nexus also operates a digital recording studio at 40 Mabel Street, Atherton; the studio is open for bookings by appointment. The organisation states it connects creative industries, organisations, communities and stakeholders across artforms and cultures within the region.
