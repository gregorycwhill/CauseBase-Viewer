Enfield Baptist Church Inc is a local Baptist church based at 1 Francis Ave, Broadview, South Australia. It holds Sunday services in person and online (Sunday 10:00am) and maintains an online archive of recorded sermons. The church lists a named Aged Care Volunteer Visitors Scheme and a program called Lifewell Ministries, and publishes upcoming public events and connection information for congregation and visitors. Volunteers participate in the aged-care visiting program; members of the public and congregation may attend services and events. Contact details and directions are provided on the church website.
