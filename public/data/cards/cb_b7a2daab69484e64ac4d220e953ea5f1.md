Landscape Recovery Foundation Ltd. is a Tasmania‑based conservation foundation active as of 2026 that aims to translate scientific research into on‑ground actions to address biodiversity loss and land and sea degradation in Tasmania (including nipaluna/Hobart and the east coast). Its work includes multidisciplinary field research on swift parrot nesting (field surveys, passive acoustic monitoring, sugar glider capture, health and diet analysis and forensic veterinary investigation), publication and dissemination of research (for example the report ‘Swift Parrots Beating the Odds’), delivery of named conservation projects (Swift Parrot Conservation; East Coast Catchments; Eucalypt Conservation; Saving Threatened Orchids), administration of the Melanie J. Kelly bursary, community clean‑up events and partnerships with land managers, NGOs, government, corporations and communities. A 2026 foundation report is cited for findings on swift parrot nesting success.
