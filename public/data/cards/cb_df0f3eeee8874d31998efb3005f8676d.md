## CauseBase summary

AEIOU Foundation’s website states the organisation was established in 2005 to provide early intervention for children aged 2–6 with an autism diagnosis. The site says AEIOU is neurodiversity-affirming, uses a mix of person‑first and identity‑first language based on feedback from a 2023 parent community study, and says it supports each child’s strengths, abilities and personal goals. Contact details on the site include a phone number (1300 273 435), an email address (info@aeiou.org.au), office hours and a street address (Level 1, 15 Astor Terrace, Spring Hill QLD 4000).
