It runs regular circus classes for children and adults, delivers school-holiday workshops and full-day holiday programs, and stages public circus‑theatre performances through a named CircoBats Performance Troupe (including a Fringe presentation titled 'The Dreamer'). The organisation advertises term timetables, enrolment dates and holiday program schedules for varying age groups and skill levels at its Adelaide venue.
