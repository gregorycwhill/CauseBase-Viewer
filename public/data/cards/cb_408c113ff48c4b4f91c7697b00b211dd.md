## CauseBase summary

Defence Force Welfare Association - National Inc (DFWA) describes itself on its website as "an independent voice for Australian Defence Force members, veterans, and their families." The site lists specific advocacy areas including ADF pay and conditions of service, rehabilitation of injured veterans, compensation for service injuries, retirement and superannuation, improving service delivery, and veteran recognition. DFWA publishes a newsletter and a membership magazine called Camaraderie (Vol. 57, Nos. 1 and 2 cited in 2026) and invites people to subscribe or download editions online. The organisation runs and promotes surveys related to the WRA 2026–29 process (a Pre-Offer Survey posted 28 May 2026 and a Post-Offer Survey posted 02 Aug 2026) and has posted media releases calling on ADF members to respond to a proposed 3% pay offer. DFWA states it is funded by branch membership fees and donations. Regulatory records include an ACNC extract and an AIS entry for 01/07/2022–30/06/2023 reporting revenue of AUD 80,194 and total expenses of AUD 84,179.
