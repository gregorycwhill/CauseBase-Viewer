The Water Well Project Limited is an Australian community organisation that runs health education sessions for people from migrant and refugee backgrounds and broader community groups. It delivers in-school sessions for students, public or community sessions on navigating the Australian healthcare system, preventative health and healthy eating, and recruits volunteer healthcare professionals to support delivery. Community representatives and participants quoted by the organisation say the sessions “have improved health literacy and built confidence in navigating the Australian healthcare system” and report learning about preventative care and affordable ambulance cover. Volunteer healthcare professionals describe the experience as improving their understanding of the health needs of people from migrant and refugee backgrounds. (Sources: organisation website and regulatory filings.)
