## CauseBase summary

The Water Well Project Limited is described on its website as running community-facing health education sessions for people from migrant and refugee backgrounds and other community participants. Website excerpts quote community members and teachers saying sessions create a space to discuss sensitive health topics, improve health literacy, and build confidence in navigating the Australian healthcare system; specific participant comments mention learning about preventative healthcare, healthier food choices and ambulance cover. A volunteer healthcare professional quoted on the site says volunteering helped them understand migrant and refugee health needs. Teacher feedback in the excerpts refers to high student engagement and many questions during sessions. The organisation’s ACNC Annual Information Statement for 01/07/2022–30/06/2023 records revenue of AUD 327,452 and total expenses of AUD 399,752 for that period. The available evidence consists of organisation-published web quotes and the ACNC AIS financial extract.
