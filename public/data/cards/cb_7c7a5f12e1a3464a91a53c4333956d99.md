## CauseBase summary

Institute of Public Works Engineering Australasia (NSW Division) Ltd (IPWEA NSW & ACT) is a professional association for public works and local government engineering professionals operating in New South Wales and the Australian Capital Territory. It delivers accredited qualifications, short courses and workshops as a Registered Training Organisation (RTO 90174) and provides NSW Government‑subsidised training via Smart and Skilled (318282). IPWEA NSW & ACT hosts technical events, forums and conferences — including a 2026 Public Works AI Forum and a Development Engineering Forum — and undertakes advocacy and policy activities. Participation options described include joining as a member, registering for and attending events, and enrolling in accredited or short courses.
