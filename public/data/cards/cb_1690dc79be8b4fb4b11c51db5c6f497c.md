## CauseBase summary

The organisation’s website describes Dieback Working Group Inc as a WA‑based group focused on Phytophthora cinnamomi (Phytophthora Dieback). The website states the group was founded in 1996 by Dr Ian Colquhoun and is marking 30 years in 2026. Services listed on the site include Green Card Training (Dieback Biosecurity Awareness Training), which the website says is recognised by WA government authorities; a public biosecurity campaign called “Arrive Clean – Leave Clean”; a Find an Expert service; Treatment News; FAQs; and publication of a Managing Dieback Best Practice Guideline. The site promotes an annual conference (DIG) with dates listed for 2026 and references past DIG highlights. The website frames Phytophthora Dieback as a widespread concern in southern Western Australia and lists stakeholders involved in management—government, industry, landholders, research organisations, universities and community groups—while drawing attention to risks from tourism and outdoor recreation (for example mountain biking, geocaching and four‑wheel driving). The organisation’s 2022–23 AIS shows revenue of AUD 276,556 and expenses of AUD 90,061 for the year 01/07/2022–30/06/2023.
