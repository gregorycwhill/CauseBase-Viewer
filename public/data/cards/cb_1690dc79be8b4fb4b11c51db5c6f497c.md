## CauseBase summary

Dieback Working Group Inc is an organisation operating in southern Western Australia that focuses on managing Phytophthora Dieback, a plant disease caused by Phytophthora cinnamomi. It delivers biosecurity awareness training (Green Card Training) recognised by WA government authorities, runs a public conference series (DIG) and other public events, and promotes an ‘Arrive Clean – Leave Clean’ public education campaign. The group publishes guidance and information (Managing Dieback Best Practice Guideline, Treatment News, FAQs) and operates an information referral service (Find an Expert). Its stated stakeholders include government and industry agencies, landholders and land managers, community groups and outdoor recreation users; its work also relates to native plants and regional biodiversity in southern Western Australia. Evidence for these activities comes from the organisation’s website and its 2023 ACNC information statement.
