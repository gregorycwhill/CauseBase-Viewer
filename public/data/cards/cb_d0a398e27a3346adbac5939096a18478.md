## CauseBase summary

Australian Indigenous Governance Institute Limited (AIGI) is described on its website as an Indigenous‑led centre for governance knowledge and professional development. The site states AIGI “connects Indigenous Australians to world‑class governance practice, resources and professional development” and hosts an online library of practical resources for Indigenous organisations (examples dated 2012–2025). AIGI lists recurring professional development activities: public events and workshop series (examples titled “Leading from the Chair”, “Running Effective and Culturally Respectful meeting”, and “Plan and Prep for your meetings” with dates in 2026), the Indigenous Governance Awards, and tailored professional development designed with client organisations. The website also offers governance reviews described as “comprehensive and independent” advisory services. The organisation’s 2022–23 ACNC AIS report records revenue of AUD 1,599,914 and total expenses of AUD 1,534,271 for the year ending 30 June 2023. The website includes statements about supporting self‑determination and bringing Indigenous ways of thinking to mainstream governance; these are presented as the organisation’s claims.
