Australian Indigenous Governance Institute Limited (AIGI) is an Indigenous-led not-for-profit that supports governance among Indigenous peoples and organisations. Registered in Queensland, AIGI works with Indigenous Australians across the country. Its core activities include maintaining an extensive online library of practical governance resources, running public professional-development events (for example Leading from the Chair and Meeting Management series), designing and delivering tailored training with client organisations, conducting comprehensive governance reviews and advisory engagements, and administering the Indigenous Governance Awards. AIGI describes its approach as working alongside Indigenous peoples to align governance practice with cultural legitimacy and self-determination.
