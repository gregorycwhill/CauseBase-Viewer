Northern Rivers Animal Services (NRAS) is a self-funded, not-for-profit registered charity based in Ballina that rescues abandoned, abused, lost and unwanted dogs and cats across the Northern Rivers region of New South Wales. NRAS operates a rescue shelter at 61 Piper Drive, Ballina, places animals in foster care and runs monthly Adoption Days (first Saturday, 10:00–13:00) where prospective adopters can meet animals and access services such as microchipping, nail clipping and hydrobathing. Animals are desexed, microchipped, vaccinated and vet-checked prior to adoption. NRAS also runs a subsidised desexing program, operates an op shop in Ballina and stages community events (including Dogs Day Out and a pet calendar competition). Volunteer roles include foster caring, shelter and shop staffing, transport and general maintenance.
