## CauseBase summary

Northern Rivers Animal Services (NRAS) is a self‑funded, not‑for‑profit registered charity established in 2001 that describes itself as rescuing abandoned, abused, lost and unwanted dogs and cats in the Northern Rivers region of New South Wales. NRAS operates a rescue shelter at 61 Piper Drive, Ballina and runs monthly Adoption Days on the first Saturday of each month (10:00–13:00) where animals available for adoption are presented alongside a pop‑up merchandise store, a community barbecue and low‑cost services such as $10 microchipping, nail clipping and hydrobathing. The organisation advertises that animals remain with foster carers or at the shelter until rehoming; it states all animals are desexed, microchipped, vet checked and vaccinated before adoption. NRAS lists a subsidised desexing program, a Ballina op shop and community events (including an annual Dogs Day Out and a pet calendar competition). The website invites people to become foster carers, volunteer at the shelter or shop, assist with animal transport, garden and maintenance, event staffing or office duties, and notes a nominal annual membership fee and options for donations including gifts in wills.
