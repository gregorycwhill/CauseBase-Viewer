## CauseBase summary

Australian Design Centre (ADC) is presented on its website as a Sydney-based organisation that curates and tours design exhibitions, operates a retail Object Shop and publishes research into design. The ADC website states it presented “over 175 exhibitions in Sydney” in the last decade, claiming the works of “13,200+ artists” were shown; it also says Object Space (a Williams Street window gallery) hosted 53 exhibitions with 104 artists since 2017. The site describes “ADC On Tour,” saying that “since the 1970s ADC has toured nationally” to provide regional Australia with access to exhibitions. The Object Shop is described as supporting “over 150 leading and emerging Australian designer makers.” The website lists government partners including NSW Government, City of Sydney and Australian Government (Visions of Australia). The ACNC AIS 2023 extract in the evidence pack reports revenue of AUD 1,403,560 and total expenses of AUD 1,364,655 for 2023. The website also contains an Announcement Board item about a Board decision on ADC’s future and a December 2025 feedback survey about the impact of “ADC closing.”
