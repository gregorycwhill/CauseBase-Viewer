Australian Design Centre is a Sydney-based organisation that curates and presents design exhibitions and related programs. It operates a Williams Street window gallery called Object Space, runs an Object Shop retail outlet that supports more than 150 Australian designer makers, and operates ADC On Tour travelling exhibitions that it says have toured nationally since the 1970s to provide regional Australia with access. ADC publishes Object Digital research into object and design disciplines and commissions engagement research, including a 2025 feedback survey about the impact of ADC closing. Over the last decade the centre reports it presented more than 175 exhibitions in Sydney featuring 13,200+ artists; since 2017 Object Space staged 53 exhibitions with 104 artists. People can attend exhibitions and tours, visit the shop, exhibit in Object Space, attend events and workshops, or subscribe to ADC communications.
