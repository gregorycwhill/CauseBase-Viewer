Alcoholics Anonymous, Perth, Western Australia (Aa Service Council For Western Australia) is a local fellowship supporting people who identify a desire to stop drinking. It maintains a staffed office in East Perth with published opening hours (Monday–Friday, 9am–4.30pm) and operates a 24‑hour helpline. The group compiles and publishes meeting lists and a calendar of events (including open and online meetings), provides resources for new members (pamphlets, personal stories, talks and workshops), and publishes or links to AA literature and Serenity magazine. The organisation also maintains a safety policy, code of conduct and incident reporting forms. Open meetings are available to anyone interested in AA’s programme of recovery; the site includes specific contact options for fly‑in/fly‑out workers.
