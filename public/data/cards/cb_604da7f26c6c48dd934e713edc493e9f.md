## CauseBase summary

Environmental Justice Australia is a public interest legal organisation based in Victoria that works across Australia. It provides legal advice and pro‑bono representation, runs court cases and high‑stakes legal interventions, and conducts advocacy campaigns and legal investigations. The organisation represents communities affected by environmental harm and people with the least power, including providing legal support to Traditional Owners seeking to protect Country and Culture. It pursues matters such as climate litigation, challenges to fossil fuel expansion, and cases to defend forests, rivers and threatened wildlife. The organisation also publishes legal updates and runs public webinars and subscription updates for people who want to follow its work.
