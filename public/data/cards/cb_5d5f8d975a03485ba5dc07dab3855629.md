Italian Australian Welfare Association (Granite Belt) Inc, trading as IAWA Home Care, is a registered aged care provider serving Stanthorpe and the Granite Belt region in Queensland (information current to 2026). It accepts referrals for Support at Home Packages through My Aged Care and provides services under the Commonwealth Home Support Program. The organisation operates a Granite Belt Ethnic Health Liaison Service (noted as government-funded) and describes itself as a grassroots, community-based not-for-profit that historically focused on Italian-born people and people of Italian descent and on people from non-English-speaking backgrounds, while now providing services to residents of the Granite Belt irrespective of background. IAWA also publishes and sells a cookbook and maintains a whistleblower/protected-disclosure contact for home care concerns.
