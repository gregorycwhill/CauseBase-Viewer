## CauseBase summary

The Italian Australian Welfare Association (Granite Belt) Inc (trading as IAWA Home Care) is described on its website as an approved, registered Aged Care Provider (Provider No. 26626) based in Stanthorpe and the Granite Belt region of Queensland. The organisation states it accepts referrals for Support at Home Packages through the federal My Aged Care gateway and offers services under the Commonwealth Home Support Program. The website characterises the group as a grass‑roots, community‑based not‑for‑profit that aims to reduce cultural and language barriers; it says its earlier services focused on Italian‑born people and Italian descendants and others from non‑English speaking backgrounds, and that it has since expanded services “to everyone (irrespective of your background).” The site also references a Granite Belt Ethnic Health Liaison Service funded by the Australian Government, a published IAWA cookbook available for purchase, and a whistleblower contact (IAWA Home Care Delegate, Eddie Stringer). Financial data filed in the ACNC AIS for 2022–23 list revenue of AUD 1,148,626 and expenses of AUD 1,018,436 for the reporting year.
