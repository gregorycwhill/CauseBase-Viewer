## CauseBase summary

The organisation’s website identifies itself as the Russian Imperial Order of St Michael the Archangel and describes an Australian Division of a dynastic Order of knighthood “instituted in 1988.” The website presents governance material (a constitution and a 2012 statute), a “Join now” membership prompt, a photo gallery and a contact/enquiry form. The site states that a “Foundation Contribution” is applied to running the Order and to “humanitarian activities,” and that an “Annual Oblation” supports charitable works designated by “Her Imperial Highness” through the Australian Division Office.
