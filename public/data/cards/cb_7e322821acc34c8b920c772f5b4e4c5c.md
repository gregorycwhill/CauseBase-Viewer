## CauseBase summary

Adelaide Symphony Orchestra Pty Ltd is a professional symphony orchestra based in Adelaide, South Australia. It presents a season of concerts at venues including Adelaide Town Hall, Elder Hall and the Festival Theatre, and undertakes regional touring and community concerts in South Australian towns such as Peterborough and Clare. The ASO runs learning and schools programmes and community projects (for example Floods of Fire), collaborates with festivals and other companies, and produces digital and broadcast programming including an online concert platform and mini‑recitals. Public participation opportunities include ticketed concerts, community workshops and participatory projects, and online attendance; the organisation also references volunteers and other supporter involvement.

## Activities

- Symphony and concert series at Adelaide Town Hall, Elder Hall and Festival Theatre
- Regional touring and community concerts in South Australia (Peterborough, Clare, mid‑north)
