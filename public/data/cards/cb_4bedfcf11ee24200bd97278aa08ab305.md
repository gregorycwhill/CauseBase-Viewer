## CauseBase summary

FORM Building a State of Creativity Limited (FORM) is an arts organisation based in Perth that works across Western Australia, including activity in Port Hedland (Pilbara). As of 2026 it undertakes public art and public-space projects, runs FORM Gallery exhibitions and Spinifex Hill Studio activities, delivers creative learning programmes and cultural tourism experiences, and offers arts and cultural consultancy. FORM also stages events, artist callouts and public exhibitions and describes partnerships with Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander organisations. Its stated mission is to develop a vibrant creative economy for the benefit and wellbeing of Western Australian communities.
