## CauseBase summary

The ACNC register lists the legal name Darwin Volunteer Unit (NTES) Association Incorporation. The Northern Territory Emergency Service (NTES) website says NTES is part of NT Police, Fire and Emergency Services and is affiliated with the Australia-wide State Emergency Service. The website states NTES is mostly volunteer-based and describes its responsibilities as facilitating emergency management planning, raising public awareness about disasters and providing a response capability for emergency events through dedicated volunteers. It identifies floods, storms and cyclones as event types and publishes public safety and flood preparedness material (advice on what to pack and what to stock). The site includes a community engagement page and notes volunteers come “from all walks of life.” Contact details shown for assistance are 132 500 (and 000 for emergencies). The site also references a 50-year celebration of NTES. The provided sources are the organisation’s website and an ACNC register extract; no additional operational or financial documents were provided in the evidence pack.
