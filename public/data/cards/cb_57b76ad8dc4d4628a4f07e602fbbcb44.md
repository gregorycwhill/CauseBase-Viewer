Darwin Volunteer Unit (NTES) Association Incorporation is the volunteer component of the Northern Territory Emergency Service (NTES), part of NT Police, Fire and Emergency Services, operating across the Northern Territory. NTES maintains a volunteer-led response capability for floods, storms and cyclones, facilitates emergency management planning, and provides public information and community engagement on preparedness and public safety. Volunteers from a range of backgrounds support operational response activities and local community awareness initiatives, and NTES is affiliated with the Australia-wide State Emergency Service. The service publishes flood preparedness advice and practical guidance for residents and promotes community engagement in resilience and preparedness work.
