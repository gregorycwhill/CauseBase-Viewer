## CauseBase summary

Stallard Meek Flightpath Foundation (SMFF) is presented on its website as a not-for-profit that raises funds and awareness for organisations, cultural groups and underrepresented communities to ‘better their built environments, strengthen their social infrastructures and/or support their defined ethos’. The foundation describes offering opportunities for architectural and design industry professionals to undertake research projects, and to participate in collaboration, mentorships and sponsorships for persons and organisations within the art, architecture and design sectors. Recent project listings on the site include sponsorship of the NAWIC International Women’s Day 2025 event, where SMFF is named as a Networking Event Sponsor for the Post Event Networking Drinks at the Malt Shovel Taphouse on 28 February 2025 and states an interest in advocating for women in the construction industry. SMFF also describes an active collaboration with Adelaide Botanic High School: tailoring the Year 11–12 Architectural Specialisation Programme, participating in one to two lessons each term, and teaching topics such as site analysis strategies, architectural typologies, ideation and design principles, graphic communication methods and studio-style pin-up presentations. The ACNC register entry for the organisation was observed but no financial details were supplied in the provided evidence.
