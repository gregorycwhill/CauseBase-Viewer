## CauseBase summary

Stallard Meek Flightpath Foundation (SMFF) is a not-for-profit based in South Australia that raises funds and awareness for organisations, cultural groups and underrepresented communities with a focus on built environments and social infrastructure. SMFF provides opportunities for architectural and design professionals to undertake research projects, and offers collaboration, mentorships and sponsorships for people and organisations in the art, architecture and design sectors. Recent activities include sponsorship of the NAWIC International Women’s Day 2025 networking event and supporting a post‑event networking function, and an active curriculum partnership with Adelaide Botanic High School’s Year 11–12 Architectural Specialisation Programme, where SMFF delivers one to two lessons per term and participates in studio‑style pin‑up presentations.
