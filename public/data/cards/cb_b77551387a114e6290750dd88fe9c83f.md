Australian Community Transport Association (ACTA) is the national peak body for community transport providers in Australia, with membership spanning all states and territories and services delivered in metropolitan, regional, rural and remote communities. ACTA represents community transport providers (including not‑for‑profits, local governments and regional providers) and undertakes advocacy and policy reform aimed at transport equity, provides sector leadership and support for service providers, runs national conferences and webinars, and coordinates the National Community Transport Pricing Model (NCTPM) pilot. ACTA’s stated beneficiaries include older Australians, people with disability, carers and others facing transport disadvantage who use community transport services.
