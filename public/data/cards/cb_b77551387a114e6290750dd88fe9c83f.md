## CauseBase summary

The Australian Community Transport Association (ACTA) presents itself as a national membership body for community transport providers, stating membership from “100+” organisations across all states and territories. ACTA’s website describes its stated activities as policy reform, sector leadership, advocacy for transport equity and support for service providers; it publishes news about webinars, a national conference and a National Community Transport Pricing Model (NCTPM) pilot run with iMove and the University of South Australia. The site reports aggregate figures attributed to its membership: 6.05 million trips per year, 261,800 clients served and 104.5 million kilometres of travel. ACTA’s online description characterises “community transport” services (as delivered by members) as person‑centred transport for older Australians, people with disability, carers and others facing transport disadvantage across metropolitan, regional, rural and remote communities. The website invites organisations, individuals and community groups to become members and includes testimonials from member organisations and pilot participants.
