## CauseBase summary

Fight Cancer Foundation (formerly Bone Marrow Donor Institute) is described on its website as a national charity. The organisation’s 2015 annual report outlines multiple service streams. An education support program called Back on Track places specialist Education Advisors (trained teachers) in children’s hospitals to provide one-to-one tutoring, group learning sessions, Individual Learning Plans, liaison with schools and clinical teams, and transition planning for learners receiving cancer treatment; the report gives a detailed daily schedule of advisor activities. The report also describes three accommodation centres—Hilltop (Albury), John Opie House (Hobart) and BMDI Rotary House (North Melbourne)—that provide short-term, subsidised accommodation to patients and families who must travel for treatment; house managers handle bookings, patient travel assistance invoicing, waiting lists and on-site issues. The report states involvement with the BMDI Cord Blood Bank in Melbourne (reporting more than 12,700 cord blood units stored and 525 releases for transplant) and ongoing links with the Australian Bone Marrow Donor Registry via a board position. The organisation’s materials note volunteer directors, volunteers and patrons, and describe events, fundraising and research scholarships in support of these programs.
