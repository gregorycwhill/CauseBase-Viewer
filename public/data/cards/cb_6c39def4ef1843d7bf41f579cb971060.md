## CauseBase summary

Bone Marrow Donor Institute (trading as Fight Cancer Foundation) is an Australian charity first established in 1989 that provides practical support to people affected by cancer and their families. It operates nationally with services based in Albury, North Melbourne (BMDI Rotary House), Hobart (John Opie House), Sydney and Adelaide. Its concrete work includes a hospital-based education support program for children (Back on Track), short-term accommodation for patients and families who must travel for treatment, ongoing support of the BMDI Cord Blood Bank in Melbourne, research scholarships and funding, and fundraising and public events. The organisation also liaises with hospitals and education departments to coordinate services and deploys volunteers and volunteer directors in program delivery.
