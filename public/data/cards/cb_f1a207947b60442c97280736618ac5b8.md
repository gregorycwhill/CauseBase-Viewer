## CauseBase summary

ROLDA Ltd is an Australian-registered animal welfare organisation that raises funds in NSW and channels support to rescue work in Romania and other countries. It rescues and shelters dogs and cats from streets, puppy mills and disaster or conflict zones; provides food, shelter, urgent veterinary treatment and rehabilitation; evacuates animals from war and disaster areas; and rehomes animals in Europe or places them in foster care. ROLDA also buys and develops shelter land (including a cage-free sanctuary project), supports independent local rescue teams, and runs community education and early-intervention programmes that help vulnerable pet owners keep animals with their families.

## Activities

- Rescue dogs and cats from streets, puppy mills and disaster/conflict zones
- Provide shelter, food, warmth and urgent veterinary treatment
- Rehabilitate animals and place them in foster care or rehome in Europe
- Evacuate animals from conflict zones and disaster sites
- Buy and develop land and shelter facilities (e.g. renovations near Galaţi, Little Haven sanctuary project)
- Provide community education programmes about humane treatment and responsible ownership
- Provide support (food and medical assistance) to vulnerable pet owners to prevent abandonment
- Support independent local rescue teams ('ROLDA Heroes')

## Beneficiaries

- Vulnerable pet owners and disadvantaged community members who receive food or medical assistance for pets
