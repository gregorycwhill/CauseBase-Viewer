## CauseBase summary

ROLDA Ltd (registered in NSW) presents itself as an Australian fundraising and support entity for ROLDA’s international animal-welfare work. The organisation's website says it funds and supports rescue, shelter, rehabilitation and rehoming for dogs and cats in Romania and elsewhere in Europe; evacuates, feeds and treats animals affected by the war in Ukraine; and provides emergency aid for animals in other crises such as an earthquake in Venezuela. The website gives concrete examples: rescuing dogs from puppy mills, urgent veterinary surgery and foster care for animals injured by shelling, buying land and renovating dog housing near a shelter in Galaţi, and planning a cage-free sanctuary called Little Haven. It also describes keeping families together by supplying pet food and medical assistance to vulnerable owners, running education programmes in communities and schools, and supporting independent rescue teams it calls “ROLDA Heroes.” The site includes fundraising and public engagement activities such as a 20th‑anniversary challenge and donor appeals. The supplied evidence is the organisation website and an ACNC register extract; the website’s statements are organisation-authored and no financial statements or independent evaluations were supplied in the evidence pack.
