## CauseBase summary

Agency Projects Limited (Agency) states on its website that it celebrates and promotes Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander art, culture and people on a local, national and international scale. The site lists a range of concrete activities: First Nations‑led exhibitions of urban, regional and remote artists; art and built‑environment commissions; forums and public talks under the UNTOLD program; digital initiatives including the UPLANDS mapping resource, large‑scale art projections and artist video portraits; publications under Agency Editions; and a residencies program launched in 2023 called Agency AiR. Agency’s event programming examples on the site include a 2024 Rubbish on the Shore Conference on Country, Cultural Conversations at Garma, International NAIDOC Week programming with embassies, and curated projects and book launches in partnership with organisations such as Martumili Artists. The website also describes international project work with the Department of Foreign Affairs and Trade and overseas institutions. The site invites one‑off donations.
