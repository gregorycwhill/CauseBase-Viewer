The Corporation Of The Trustees Of The Order Of The Sisters Of Mercy In Queensland is the corporate entity for the Sisters of Mercy, Brisbane Congregation. It operates in Queensland, with activity linked to Brisbane and Aubigny. When supported, the organisation publishes news and announcements on its website and organises commemorative gatherings, farewell events and anniversary celebrations for congregation members.
