## CauseBase summary

APNIC Foundation Limited is an Australia-registered organisation (Queensland) that supports internet and digital development across the Asia Pacific. In 2025 it ran grant programs (including ISIF Asia, Trust Discretionary Fund and Community Assistance), directly implemented projects such as school connectivity and M-root deployments, and delivered training through instructor-led workshops and self-paced APNIC Academy courses. It also convenes events and regional schools (for example SOI Asia and APIE) and works with partner organisations on measurement, infrastructure and techno-policy activities. In 2025 the Foundation reports reaching 30 economies, training 11,533 people, and directly reaching about 196,700 individuals; specific project examples include RISE‑PH connecting 53 school sites (an estimated 80,000–100,000 students) and the Switch! cohort of 150 women and gender-diverse technical practitioners.
