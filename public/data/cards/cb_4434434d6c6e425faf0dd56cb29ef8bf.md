## CauseBase summary

The APNIC Foundation Limited is a Queensland-registered organisation that the Foundation’s 2025 annual report describes as an Asia Pacific digital development organisation. The report states the Foundation invested USD 10 million in 2025 across three strategic pillars (Building Capabilities, Driving Digital Innovation, Supporting Digital Transformation) and says it reached communities and practitioners in 30 economies. Reported 2025 outputs include 11,533 people trained, more than 196,700 individuals said to have directly benefited from digital inclusion efforts, and support for digital transformation work in three economies. Program examples named in the report and on the website include ISIF Asia (29 grants across 19 economies; USD 2.31 million in grants in 2025), Switch! (a cohort of 150 women and gender-diverse participants across six Southeast Asian economies), Digital Leap South Asia (DLSA in three South Asian economies) and the RISE‑PH ISIF grantee that the report says connected 53 school sites (51 public schools and 2 teacher‑training colleges; an estimated 80,000–100,000 students). The report and website describe partnerships with APNIC and Keio University, Foundation-led infrastructure activities (M-root deployments, local infrastructure sites), instructor-led and self-paced training, and public events such as SOI Asia and summits.
