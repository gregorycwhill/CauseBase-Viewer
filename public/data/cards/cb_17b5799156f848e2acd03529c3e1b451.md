Guildhouse (legal name: Craftsouth: Centre For Contemporary Craft & Design Inc) is a South Australian arts organisation supporting visual artists, craft makers and designers. It operates across South Australia and beyond and is supported by the South Australian Government through CreateSA and by the Visual Arts, Craft and Design Partnership Framework. Guildhouse delivers fellowships, peer-to-peer mentorships, direct artist commissions, professional development workshops, studio residencies and tailored professional services; it presents work to public audiences, issues calls for participation and publishes artist toolkits. The organisation also undertakes sector development activities including advocacy and targeted research, and maintains a membership of more than 1,200 practising artists.
