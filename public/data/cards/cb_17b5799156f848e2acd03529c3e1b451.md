## CauseBase summary

Regulatory records list the legal name as Craftsouth: Centre For Contemporary Craft & Design Inc, while the organisation’s website presents itself as Guildhouse. The website describes programs and services aimed at practising visual artists, craft makers and designers, and a membership of “more than 1200 practising artists across South Australia and beyond.” Concrete activities listed on the site include fellowships, peer‑to‑peer mentorships, direct artist commissions, professional development workshops, studio residencies, tailored professional services and connections to paid opportunities. The organisation also describes presenting work to audiences in traditional and non‑traditional spaces (exhibitions/public programs), publishing artist toolkits, issuing calls for participation and contributing to sector development through advocacy, targeted research and capacity building. The site names support from the South Australian Government (CreateSA) and the Visual Arts, Craft and Design Partnership Framework. Financial reporting for 2023 shows revenue and expenses reported in the AIS. The website language reflects the organisation’s own description of these activities; available evidence does not independently verify operational details or the extent of services beyond South Australia.
