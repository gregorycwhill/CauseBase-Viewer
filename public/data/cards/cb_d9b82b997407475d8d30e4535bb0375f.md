## CauseBase summary

The Healing Foundation (registered in the ACT per ACNC records) describes itself as a national Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander organisation that works with Stolen Generations survivors, their families and communities. Its website states it provides a platform to amplify survivors’ voices, creates places of safety where survivors and families can speak and tell their own stories, and supports survivor-led, community-led healing initiatives. The site lists more than 50 downloadable resources (fact sheets, reports, toolkits and videos), runs online events and public forums, and in 2026 announced a $200,000 grants program for Stolen Generations truth‑telling projects. The organisation also publishes policy material and submissions and calls for systemic changes (for example, an Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander aged care commissioner and a national plan framed as From Sorry to Action). ACNC annual information for 2022–23 reports revenue of AUD 9,041,707 and total expenses of AUD 5,783,801. News and resource pages on the website record recent leadership and program updates. Descriptions of activities, reach and impact in this summary are taken from the organisation’s own website and available ACNC financial extracts.
