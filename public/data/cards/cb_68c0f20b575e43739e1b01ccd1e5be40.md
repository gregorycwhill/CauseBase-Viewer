Safe Living Australia Ltd is a Public Benevolent Institution based in the Perth metropolitan area that provides housing and residential care for people who need safe accommodation. It operates a portfolio of properties across the Perth metro area and places occupants by referral, working with government departments, agencies, support coordinators, case managers and community programs. Its services include short- and long-term housing, urgent housing placements, trauma‑informed residential care for children and young people, and housing options for young people transitioning from care. Volunteers take part in prioritising housing placements and the organisation invites client feedback and partnership input; individual placements are reviewed after around six months.
