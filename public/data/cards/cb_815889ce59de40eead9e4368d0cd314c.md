# Rossmoyne Kindergarten Association (Inc.)

Rossmoyne Kindergarten Association (Inc.) is a community kindergarten in Rossmoyne, City of Canning, Western Australia. It operates two kindergarten classes with a maximum of 20 children per class for children who are four years old by 30 June in the year they attend. The kindergarten occupies purpose-built facilities leased from the City of Canning, with teachers appointed by the Department of Education and management conducted by a parent committee. On-site provision includes an “Enchanted Garden” nature play area with a water pump, sandpits, forts, poultry and small animals, and vegetable patches. The parent committee manages enrolment administration (applications completed in person at Rossmoyne Primary School), coordinates volunteer involvement, and publishes a 360-degree virtual tour and information on fees and intake timing.
