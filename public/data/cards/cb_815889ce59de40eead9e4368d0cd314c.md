## CauseBase summary

Rossmoyne Kindergarten Association (Inc.) operates Rossmoyne Community Kindergarten, described on its website as a community-run kindergarten leased from the City of Canning and operating since 1969. The website states the kindergarten has two classes with a maximum of 20 children each (a stated staff-to-children ratio of 2:20) and that teachers are appointed by the Department of Education while the service is managed by a parent committee. The site describes an on-site “Enchanted Garden” nature play area with a water pump, sandpits, forts, chickens, rabbits, guinea pigs and vegetable patches and says these features support children’s cognitive and emotional development. Enrolment is described as an in-person, two-step process through Rossmoyne Primary School with an intake preference for local-area residents and siblings; the site states a maximum of 40 kindergarten places per year. The website lists the current cost as A$820 per year (A$205 per term), notes the service relies on community support including grants, donations and volunteers, and offers both an on-site tour and a 360-degree virtual tour. ACNC extracts and the AIS 2023 are listed as regulatory sources; AIS 2023 reports revenue of A$48,227 and expenses of A$49,568.
