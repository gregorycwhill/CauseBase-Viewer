Haileybury Rendall School is a co‑educational day, boarding and online school based in Darwin, Northern Territory, Australia. It educates children from an Early Learning Centre to Year 12, offers boarding for Years 7–12 to domestic and international students, and provides an online program for Years 5–12. The school runs a compulsory Experiential Outdoor Education programme in remote environments and delivers the VCE curriculum in senior years. On campus it maintains sporting and performing‑arts facilities available for hire and a Health Centre for boarding students. The school also states a commitment to educating Aboriginal students from remote Australian communities and public programming such as open days, shows and regional event participation is listed on its events calendar.
