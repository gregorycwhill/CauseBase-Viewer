## CauseBase summary

Haileybury Rendall School is a school based in Darwin, Northern Territory. The school’s website states it provides education from an Early Learning Centre (ages 3–5) through to Year 12, and offers day, boarding and online delivery: the site describes Years 7–12 boarding (for domestic and international students) and an online program for Years 5–12. The website describes a compulsory Experiential Learning outdoor education program that includes activities in remote environments, a teaching framework that uses an Explicit Teaching Model in primary years and VCE in senior years, and a stated commitment to educating Aboriginal students from remote Australian communities. Facilities described on the site include three ovals, basketball and netball courts, a large outdoor gymnasium, a 25-metre outdoor swimming pool, performing-arts spaces (Dumabudla Centre, auditorium, music rooms, drama and dance studio), a modern library/resource space and an on-campus Health Centre for boarding students. The website also notes affiliation with Haileybury campuses in Melbourne and a Haileybury presence in Tianjin, China, and claims the school produced the highest ATAR result in the Northern Territory in 2025. An ACNC AIS extract for 2023 records revenue of AUD 33,396,968 and total expenses of AUD 31,041,515.
