## CauseBase summary

Kosciuszko to Coast (K2C) is described on its website as a partnership of fourteen organisations that “deliver better natural resource management and environment services and learn from land owners and managers, landcare groups, and community.” The site lists multiple concrete activities: a project titled “Being a Good Neighbour to the Bush for the Yass Gorge” (noting assistance from the NSW Government’s Environmental Trust); a Land for Wildlife scheme and property assessments delivered in south‑east New South Wales and offered to rural lessees in the Australian Capital Territory; workshops run under the Saving our Species program focused on habitat for Scarlet Robin and other vulnerable bush birds; and local events and site visits (for example, Feeling Fishy Day and a visit to Maffra Lake TSR). News items on the site name quolls and other Monaro arboreal mammals. The website frames K2C work as partnership delivery involving landholders, landcare groups and partners. The ACNC AIS 2023 extract in the evidence package reports zero revenue and zero expenses for the 2022–23 reporting year for this subject.
