The Aboriginal Legal Rights Movement (ALRM) is an Aboriginal community-controlled legal service based in Adelaide on Kaurna country that works across South Australia. ALRM pursues social justice, equality and wellbeing for Aboriginal people of South Australia, with particular focus on those detained in police custody or imprisoned. It operates a Custody Notification Service and provides criminal court services and programs for Aboriginal people across the state. ALRM also offers a Confirmation of Aboriginality service, membership and volunteering opportunities, and runs public programming such as Law Week panels. The organisation issues media releases and advocacy statements, invites applications for Aboriginal community board members and accepts tax-deductible donations to support its work.
