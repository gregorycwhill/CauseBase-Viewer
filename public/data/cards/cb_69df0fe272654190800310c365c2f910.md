## CauseBase summary

The Trustee for Invasive Species Solutions Trust (branded Centre for Invasive Species Solutions on its website) describes itself as leading “collaborative invasive species research, development and engagement” and says its purpose is to “safeguard Australia’s environment, agriculture and way of life.” The website lists national coordination roles (LandSmart Services National Coordination), named reports (WeedRemeed Impact Report 2024–25) and claims two Australian Biosecurity Awards. The site hosts or points to multiple digital platforms: weeds.org.au (weed management hub), weedscan.org.au (AI weed identification), pestsmart.org.au (codes of practice, training and resources) and feralscan.org.au (record and manage pests). The site invites subscription, highlights news and events, and includes a donate prompt. Regulatory records show the entity is registered in the Australian Capital Territory and its ACNC AIS 2022–23 figures report revenue of AUD 5,235 and total expenses of AUD 3,305 for 01/07/2022–30/06/2023. Most descriptive material in the evidence pack is from the organisation’s website; where the organisation makes factual claims these are presented as the organisation’s statements.
