Akademeia Limited (trading as Pinnacle Academic College) operates an on‑site campus in Kallangur, Queensland and provides distance and blended primary education for Years 1–6. It offers a fully online Bronze package, Silver and Gold blended packages that combine online study with scheduled on‑campus days, workshops, excursions and on‑campus activities, and a full‑time on‑campus option (currently full, with a waitlist). The Gold package lists an intensive swimming program. The organisation states it supports families across Australia and provides learning support and educational resources. Enrolment options include fully online, blended attendance with on‑campus days, or full‑time on‑site study; prospective families are invited to book a tour or submit an enquiry to receive fees and enrolment information.
