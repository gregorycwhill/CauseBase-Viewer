## CauseBase summary

Angel Gowns Australia Incorporated is a volunteer-run charity that repurposes donated wedding and evening-wear fabrics into garments and related items for bereaved families, hospitals and funeral homes. The organisation’s 2021 annual report states it had over 370 volunteers across all states and territories, organised seamstress, tutu-maker and de-constructor roles, and named a management committee and state coordinators. Program activity described in that report includes: handcrafting Angel Gown garments and micro-gowns; producing more than 60 tutus in the past 12 months using salvaged tulle (stock gifted to Ronald McDonald House Perth, Perth Children’s Hospital, Royal Children’s Hospital Melbourne and Women’s and Children’s Hospital Adelaide); creating an Early Loss Pouch for babies under 20 weeks; and fulfilling special requests (report lists specific gifts such as eight poem cards with crystals, multiple micro-gown/wrap packs and larger gowns). The report records the service being stocked by 211 hospitals and 102 funeral homes and notes logistics challenges during COVID (storage access, delivery delays with TOLL/AusPost). The organisation’s 2022–23 regulatory reporting shows revenue of AUD 14,107 and expenses of AUD 23,383.

## Activities

- Handcrafting Angel Gown garments and micro-gowns from donated wedding dresses and donating them to hospitals, funeral homes and families
- Making tutus from salvaged tulle and gifting them to hospitals and children’s services
- Producing Early Loss Pouches designed for babies under 20 weeks and supplying emergency departments
- Accepting and processing special garment requests from families
- Managing logistics, storage and distribution of donated dresses and finished garments
- Fundraising through online auctions, an online shop, craft shows and donation pages
- Maintaining a database and coordinating supply with hospitals and funeral homes

## Beneficiaries

- Bereaved families who experienced stillbirth or neonatal death
- Families with babies in Neonatal Intensive Care or with life-limiting conditions
- Children in hospital who receive tutus and dress-up items
- Hospitals and funeral homes that hold and distribute garments to families

## Participation

- Volunteering as seamstresses, tutu makers, de-constructors and coordinators
- Donating wedding dresses and fabric for repurposing
- Participation in public events and craft shows and online fundraising
- Submitting special requests for garments
