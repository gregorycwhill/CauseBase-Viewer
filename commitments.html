CauseBase public data commitments

CauseBase is a provenance-preserving public data layer for Australian charities. It describes and structures public information; it does not rate or recommend charities.

  • Published CauseBase IDs are durable and never silently reassigned.
  • Historical releases remain interpretable. New observations append rather than overwrite annual history.
  • Direct observations, deterministic transformations, LLM interpretations and estimates are identified separately.
  • Missing information is not a negative finding.
  • Source-native records retain regulator semantics when a field has not been canonicalised.
  • Organisations can inspect evidence and methods and contest errors; this does not provide unilateral editorial control.

This is an early 120-subject test release. It does not represent national enriched coverage.
